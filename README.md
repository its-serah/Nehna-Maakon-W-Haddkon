# Nehna Maakon W Haddkon | نحنا معكن وحدّكن

## Project Overview

"Nehna Maakon W Haddkon" (meaning "We Are With You and By Your Side" in Arabic) is a humanitarian platform that was created to support displaced individuals and communities in Lebanon affected by the conflict. The platform served as a central hub connecting those in need with essential resources such as food, shelter, medical aid, and other vital services.

**IMPORTANT NOTICE:** This project has been discontinued. It was created to help connect people after the war in Lebanon by offering food, shelter, and other essential services. The information on this site is preserved for historical purposes but may no longer be current.

## Purpose

During the humanitarian crisis caused by the conflict in Lebanon, thousands of families were displaced from their homes, creating an urgent need for shelter, food, medical care, and basic necessities. This platform was developed to:

1. Connect displaced individuals with available shelters and schools offering refuge
2. Provide information about healthcare services and emergency medical contacts
3. Facilitate donations of essential items and supplies
4. Channel monetary donations to reliable humanitarian efforts
5. Create a centralized information hub during the crisis

## Website Sections

### Home Page (index.html)
The main landing page provides an overview of the platform's purpose and navigational buttons to all resource sections. It includes a counter tracking site visits and a form link for adding new information.

### Health Support (health.html)
This section contains information about:
- Medical initiatives and volunteer groups
- Emergency healthcare hotlines
- Regional healthcare contacts for shelter centers
- Medical clinics and services available to displaced individuals
- Mental health support services
- Special medical services (maternal, pediatric, etc.)

### Items & Supplies (items.html)
This section lists:
- Needed essential items and where to donate them
- Ongoing collection initiatives for food, clothing, and hygiene products
- Contact information for volunteer groups accepting donations
- Details about specific needs (baby supplies, medications, etc.)
- Locations accepting physical donations

### Schools & Shelters (schools.html)
This section provides:
- A comprehensive list of schools functioning as shelters
- Regional emergency contact numbers
- Shelters organized by geographic region
- Special accommodations and requirements

### Money Donations (Moneydonations.html)
This section contains:
- Verified channels for monetary donations
- Bank account details for transfers
- Mobile payment options
- GoFundMe and other fundraising campaign links
- Direct contacts for financial aid coordination

## Technical Information

The website is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It features:
- Bilingual content in both English and Arabic
- Mobile-responsive design
- Simple navigation system
- Visit counter functionality

## How to Contribute

While this project has been discontinued, the repository remains as a historical record and potential template for similar humanitarian efforts. If you wish to use this model for other humanitarian initiatives:

1. Fork the repository
2. Modify the content to suit your specific humanitarian needs
3. Update contact information and resource details
4. Deploy to your preferred hosting service
